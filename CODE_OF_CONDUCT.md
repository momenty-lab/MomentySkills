# Community conduct

MomentySkills is a place to test ideas in public and learn from disagreement. Participate with care for the people doing that work.

## Expected conduct

- Critique claims, methods, and artifacts with specific evidence.
- Describe uncertainty and mistakes plainly, including your own.
- Respect different experience levels, languages, identities, and working styles.
- Protect private information and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Make room for negative and inconclusive results.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and deliberate manipulation of evidence are not accepted. Do not pressure contributors to change or conceal an outcome.

## Enforcement

Use the repository's GitHub moderation and reporting tools to flag conduct concerns. If a concern involves repository maintainers, GitHub's platform abuse reporting remains available. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. They should explain enforcement decisions when doing so does not expose a reporter or private information.
