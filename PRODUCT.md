# MomentySkills product brief

## Purpose

MomentySkills turns development practices into small, portable Codex skills that the community can inspect, adapt, and evaluate. It serves developers who want explicit decision criteria and review gates without depending on a private workspace or application.

## Initial product

The first deliverable is the experimental `adaptive-dev-routing` skill, a configurable hypothesis for matching development tasks to available model and reasoning configurations. Bilingual onboarding, safe opt-in installation, provenance, structured experience reporting, and local validation make the hypothesis reviewable.

## Principles

- Original, understandable content with minimal dependencies.
- Productive defaults remain hypotheses until supported by evidence.
- Requested and observed configurations stay separate.
- Negative and unknown results are first-class data.
- Installation never silently replaces a same-name personal skill.
- Public artifacts exclude private rules, app source, session data, credentials, and personal information.

## Non-goals for the first release

MomentySkills does not provide a model benchmark, guarantee savings, simulate agent behavior in CI, ship a website, or govern unrelated Momenty products. Additional skills should wait for a repeatable problem and a maintainable evaluation approach.

## Success evidence

Early success means independent users can install the skill safely, understand and customize its profile, reproduce its requested routing, report observed routing honestly, and identify where the hypothesis succeeds or fails. Production effectiveness and population-level impact are currently unverified.
