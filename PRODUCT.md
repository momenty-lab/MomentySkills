# MomentySkills product brief

## Purpose

MomentySkills turns repeatable software development practices into small, portable Codex skills that people can inspect, adapt, and use without depending on a private workspace or application.

## Current collection

- `adaptive-dev-routing` helps choose model and reasoning configurations for a development task and applies explicit specification review, quality review, and repair gates.
- `development-task-handoff` preserves and verifies development state when work moves between agents, tools, sessions, or workspaces.

Bilingual onboarding, safe opt-in installation, provenance guidance, structured experience reporting, and local validation keep the collection understandable and reviewable.

## Principles

- Original, understandable content with minimal dependencies.
- Concrete capability descriptions; outcome claims remain scoped to supporting evidence.
- Requested and observed configurations stay separate.
- Negative and unknown results are first-class data.
- Installation never silently replaces a same-name personal skill.
- Public artifacts exclude private rules, app source, session data, credentials, and personal information.

## Non-goals

MomentySkills does not provide a universal model ranking, guarantee savings or quality improvements, simulate agent behavior in CI, ship a website, or govern unrelated Momenty products. New skills should address a repeatable problem and have a maintainable way to inspect their behavior.

## Success evidence

Useful evidence shows that people can install a skill safely, understand when it applies, follow or adapt its workflow, and report observable outcomes honestly. Performance, efficiency, and quality claims require measurements that identify their scope and provenance.
